The Wind dataset is an important benchmark for long sequence time-series forecasting (LSTF) in renewable energy applications. This dataset contains 48,673 observations of wind power records collected during 2020–2021 at 15-minute intervals. Each data point consists of 7 variables related to wind power, providing a multivariate time series for modeling temporal variations in wind energy generation. The dataset can be used to evaluate forecasting models on short-term fluctuations and longer-term temporal dependencies. The train/validation/test split is 70%/10%/20% in chronological order.
