The Influenza-Like Illness (ILI) dataset is a public health time-series dataset collected by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) in the United States. It records weekly influenza-like illness activity from 2002 to 2021 and contains 7 variables related to the proportion of patients exhibiting influenza-like symptoms. The dataset consists of 966 weekly observations and is characterized by strong seasonal patterns associated with recurring influenza outbreaks. It is widely used to evaluate long-term time-series forecasting models under relatively small-data settings. The train/val/test data are chronologically divided for forecasting evaluation.
