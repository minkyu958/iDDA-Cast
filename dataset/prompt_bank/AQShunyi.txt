The AQShunyi dataset contains hourly air quality and meteorological observations collected in Shunyi District, Beijing, China, from March 2013 to February 2017. The dataset includes air pollutant concentrations such as PM2.5, PM10, SO2, NO2, CO, and O3, along with meteorological variables including temperature, atmospheric pressure, dew point, rainfall, and wind speed. These time series may exhibit long-term trends, daily and seasonal patterns, sudden pollution episodes, and irregular fluctuations influenced by meteorological conditions. The forecasting task is to predict future values of the observed variables using their historical temporal patterns.
